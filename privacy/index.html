Mjudio Remix Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Mjudio Remix is a DJ app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by SSG Team. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: we do not collect any of your data. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers of ours, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app never uploads your music files or recordings; they leave your device only if you share them yourself. Your library stays on the device too.

Mjudio Remix is published by SSG Team.

2. What we collect

Nothing. SSG Team does not run a server for this app, and the app does not send your information to us or to any analytics, advertising or tracking company. The app contains no third-party code that collects data.

We do not sell, rent or share your information, because we never receive it.

3. What stays on your device

To work, the app keeps the following in its own storage on your device. It is not sent to us.

  • Your library: the tracks you add (title, artist, album, genre, year, length, artwork, file size), where each file is, your playlists, hot cues, loops and grid edits.
  • Analysis results: tempo (BPM), beat grid, key, loudness and waveform data for each track, and, on iOS 27 and macOS 27, its song structure (sections and when vocals play). They are stored by a fingerprint of the file's content so a track is analysed only once.
  • Vocal stems: when you use the vocals / instrumental control, a processed copy of the track's vocals is kept in a cache of limited size, so it does not have to be made again.
  • Recordings: mixes you record, with their tracklists. On iPhone and iPad they are in the app's folder in the Files app.
  • Copies of files shared to the app: a file that another app hands over only temporarily (for example through the share sheet) is copied into the app's folder so it keeps playing.
  • MIDI mappings: your controller mappings, saved under the controller's name.
  • Settings and purchase status: your preferences, and a copy of your PRO status so PRO works offline.

4. Your music files

You choose which files and folders the app may use, with the system file picker or by drag and drop. The app keeps a secure reference (a "security-scoped bookmark") so it can open the same files next time. It cannot see other files.

On iPhone and iPad you can also add songs from your device's music library. The system asks for your permission first. Only songs stored on your device without copy protection can be added.

5. Smart features (on your device)

On iOS 27 and macOS 27, on a device that supports Apple's on-device language model with it turned on, the app offers smart features. They run on your device:

  • Smart search turns what you type ("house around 124 in A minor") into library filters.
  • Tag cleanup suggests titles, artists and genres from messy file names. Nothing changes until you accept it.
  • Recording text suggests a title and description for a recording, from its tracklist and length. It never uses the recording's audio, its name or its file.
  • Set builder turns a described set into a suggested automix queue for you to review.

These features use track details only: title (for a file without tags, this is its file name), artist, album, genre, tempo, key and length. They never use audio, file locations or recordings. Your words and these details stay on your device.

You can turn the smart features off in the app's Settings, on the Smart Features page.

Other features process on your device without a language model:

  • Song structure (iOS 27 and macOS 27) uses the system's music analysis on your device to find sections and vocals. No audio leaves your device. You can turn it off on the Smart Features page.
  • Why this next track writes a short line from the analysis results the app already has.
  • Setlist photo import reads the text in a photo or image you pick, on your device, and matches it against your library. The image is read once and not stored. The app does not ask for access to your photo library: you pick one image, and the app receives only that image. The app does not use the camera.

6. Siri, Shortcuts and Spotlight

The app works with Siri and the Shortcuts app: for example, loading a track to a deck or starting automix. So that Siri and Spotlight can find your music, the app gives the system your tracks' title, artist, album, genre, tempo, key and length, and your playlists' names. This happens on your device. File locations and recordings are never included.

What the system does with a Siri or Spotlight request is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

To remove your library from Spotlight, turn off "Show the library in Spotlight" in the app's Settings (MIDI page, "Siri and Spotlight").

7. Purchases

Mjudio Remix PRO is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. We never see your payment details, name or Apple Account. The app receives from the App Store, on your device, which PRO plan you have and its dates, and keeps a copy so PRO works offline.

Apple may give developers sales reports and, if you chose to share them in your device's settings, crash reports and usage statistics. These are provided by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

8. Permissions

  • Music library (iPhone and iPad): to add songs from your device's music library. Asked the first time you use it.
  • Bluetooth (iPhone and iPad): only to pair a Bluetooth MIDI controller you choose.
  • Files and folders: only the ones you pick.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone. Recording captures the app's own audio output, not the microphone.

9. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it:

  • Remove a track from the library: its entry and its Spotlight item are removed. Its analysis results stay in the app's storage until the app is deleted. The music file itself is never deleted by the app.
  • Delete a recording in the Recordings list: the audio file and its tracklist are removed.
  • Files copied into the app (iPhone and iPad) can be deleted in the Files app, in the app's folder.
  • Delete the app on iPhone or iPad to remove everything it stored on the device.
  • On a Mac, deleting the app may leave its data in the app's container folder (~/Library/Containers/com.mjudio.remix). Delete that folder to remove it.

Your device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include the app's data. Those backups are handled by Apple and by you, not by us.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

11. Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with your information, we will update this policy and the date at the top before the change is released. A change that would collect data would also be shown in the app's App Store privacy details.
